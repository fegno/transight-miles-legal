Delete your account

For the Transight Miles app by Transight Systems Private Limited · iOS and Android

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your account is removed within 30 days of your request.

Option 1 — From the app

  1. Open Transight Miles and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile (top-left of the Fleet home screen), then tap Delete Account. This page opens.
  3. Tap Email a deletion request below and send the email.

Option 2 — By email

Email us from the address registered on your account, including your name, username and registered mobile number so we can find and verify the account.

Email a deletion request

Contact: support@transight.com

What is deleted

  • Your profile, login and workspace access.
  • Vehicles, drivers, FASTag and fuel card records linked to your account.
  • Photos you uploaded, and your push-notification registration.
